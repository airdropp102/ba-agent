# Eliciting Requirements (BABOK 4.1–4.5)

**Prepare (4.1):** identify purpose, source, domain, stakeholders, assumptions, intended elicitation technique and constraints.

**Conduct (4.2):** capture stakeholder statements verbatim with source ID, excerpt, date/owner when known. Distinguish observed current state, desired future state, constraints and solution suggestions. Do not conflate requester opinion with organization-wide agreement.

**Confirm (4.3):** compare extracted statements with source, list unresolved conflicts and seek source-owner confirmation. Exact quoted text is evidence of utterance, not validation of its truth.

**Communicate (4.4):** prepare concise digest listing objectives, candidate requirements, open decisions and who must answer.

**Collaborate (4.5):** record owners, pending responses and new inputs. Elicitation is iterative.

## Rules

- Raw source is immutable; append updates with provenance rather than rewriting history.
- Separate `FACT_IN_SOURCE`, `INTERPRETATION`, `PROPOSAL`, and `QUESTION`.
- For sensitive data, store only minimum needed and respect access/consent.
- If no stakeholder or goal is supplied, explicitly ask rather than guessing.

**Output:** evidence catalog, stakeholder map, business context, assumptions, questions; forward to `ba-normalizing-requirements`.
